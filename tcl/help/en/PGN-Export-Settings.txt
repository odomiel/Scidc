INDEX PGN Export Settings
TITLE PGN Export Settings

<p>
The <b>PGN Export Settings</b> control how games are written in PGN format &ndash;
both for the <a href="Database-Export-Dialog.html">database export</a> and when
copying games to the clipboard.
</p>

<p>
Among the settings are the move notation, the handling of comments and variations,
the indentation as well as additional information such as hints about move repetition
(threefold and fivefold repetition) and the 50-move rule. A preview shows the effect
of the chosen options.
</p>

<p>
The settings can be reverted to the state when the dialog was opened (<b>Revert to
initial settings</b>) or completely to the factory settings (<b>Reset to factory
settings</b>).
</p>
