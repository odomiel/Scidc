INDEX Player Dictionary
TITLE The Player Dictionary

<p>
The <b>Player Dictionary</b> is a built-in master database for chess players. It
provides verified data such as FIDE ID, title, federation, sex and ratings, and
serves as a reference work when entering and maintaining games.
</p>

<p>
A filter narrows the display &ndash; for example by organization ID, titles, rating
ranges or a period of years. The player name search is case-insensitive and supports
pattern matching (&#x21d2; <a href="Pattern-Matching.html">Pattern matching</a>).
</p>

<p>
The rating lists can be updated directly from within the application: via <b>Update
Player Lists</b> Scidc downloads the current FIDE and DWZ data one after another and
reads them in. When saving a game, the
<a href="Game-Browser.html">save game dialog</a> proposes matching entries from the
player dictionary.
</p>
