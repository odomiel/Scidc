INDEX Game history
TITLE The Game History

<p>
The <b>Game History</b> lists the most recently opened games and allows quickly
jumping back to a previously viewed game without having to search for it again in
the database.
</p>

<p>
Each entry names the game with its header data and the originating database.
Double-clicking reloads the game. The history is kept across the session, so that
recently edited games remain accessible even after a restart.
</p>
