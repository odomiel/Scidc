INDEX Crosstable
TITLE The Crosstable

<p>
The <b>Crosstable</b> presents the results of an event in a clear overview.
Depending on the tournament format, different layouts can be chosen: round-robin
(all play all), Swiss system or team match.
</p>

<p>
It shows the participants with their ratings, the individual results against each
other as well as point totals and rankings. Clicking a result in the table opens the
corresponding game. The crosstable is usually opened from the
<a href="Event-List-Window.html">event list</a>.
</p>
