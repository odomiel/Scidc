INDEX Game text window
TITLE The Game Text Window

<p>
The <b>Game Text Window</b> shows the notation of the currently loaded game: the
main line, all variations, comments, NAG symbols
(&#x21d2; <a href="NAG-Comments.html">NAG comment values</a>) and &ndash; where
present &ndash; embedded diagrams.
</p>

<p>
Clicking a move jumps to the corresponding position on the
<a href="Board-Window.html">chessboard</a>. Through the context menu and the toolbar,
variations can be collapsed and expanded, the move notation switched, and comments
edited. The presentation (font size, colors, notation style) follows the program
settings.
</p>
