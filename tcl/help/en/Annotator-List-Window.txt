INDEX Annotator list
TITLE The Annotator List

<p>
The <b>Annotator List</b> shows all annotators of the active database, that is, the
people who have annotated games, together with the number of games they annotated.
</p>

<p>
The list can be sorted and searched. Through the context menu all games of a
particular annotator can be filtered.
</p>
