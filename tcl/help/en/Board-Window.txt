INDEX Chessboard
TITLE The Chessboard Window

<p>
The <b>Chessboard Window</b> is the central display element and shows the current
position of the loaded game. Below it are navigation buttons for stepping move by
move or jumping to the start or end of the game.
</p>

<p>
Moves can be played directly on the board with the mouse; they are then entered into
the <a href="Game-Text-Window.html">game notation</a>. The appearance and behaviour
of the board &ndash; piece set, board theme, coordinate display, marker arrows and
square colors &ndash; can be adjusted through the board themes and the settings.
Markers (arrows, colored squares) are set with the
<a href="Mark-Palette-Dialog.html">square marker palette</a>.
</p>

<p>
An arbitrary starting position can be created with the
<a href="Board-Setup-Dialog.html">board setup</a>.
</p>
