The Player Card

The Player Card summarizes the data of a player at a glance. It shows, among other things, the latest and highest rating, the period of the recorded games (first and last game) as well as a results balance (wins, draws, losses, total).

In addition, the most common openings with White and with Black are listed. This provides a quick profile of a player's style and activity.

Via links, further sources can be opened, such as the FIDE rating history, the Wikipedia biography as well as library catalogs (VIAF and the German National Library). The card is usually opened by double-clicking from the player list.
