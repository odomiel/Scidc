INDEX Site List
TITLE The Site List

<p>
The <b>Site List</b> shows all venues of the active database, together with the
associated country and the number of games played there.
</p>

<p>
The list can be sorted and searched via the search field. The search is
case-insensitive and matches substrings. Through the context menu all games of a
site can be filtered.
</p>
