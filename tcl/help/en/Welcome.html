Welcome to Scidc!

Scidc is still in beta stage. Bugs and incomplete features are possible – feedback is welcome.

Important Notes

  • The Scidb format (.sci/.scv) will no longer be developed further and is frozen at its current state. The new default format is the .si5 format from Scid.
  • Support for the ChessBase format (.cbh) is also frozen at its current state and will not be extended further.
