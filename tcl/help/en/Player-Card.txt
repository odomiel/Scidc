INDEX Player Card
TITLE The Player Card

<p>
The <b>Player Card</b> summarizes the data of a player at a glance. It shows, among
other things, the latest and highest rating, the period of the recorded games (first
and last game) as well as a results balance (wins, draws, losses, total).
</p>

<p>
In addition, the most common openings with White and with Black are listed. This
provides a quick profile of a player's style and activity.
</p>

<p>
Via links, further sources can be opened, such as the FIDE rating history, the
Wikipedia biography as well as library catalogs (VIAF and the German National
Library). The card is usually opened by double-clicking from the
<a href="Player-List-Window.html">player list</a>.
</p>
