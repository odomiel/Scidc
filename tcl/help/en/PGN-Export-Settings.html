PGN Export Settings

The PGN Export Settings control how games are written in PGN format – both for the database export and when copying games to the clipboard.

Among the settings are the move notation, the handling of comments and variations, the indentation as well as additional information such as hints about move repetition (threefold and fivefold repetition) and the 50-move rule. A preview shows the effect of the chosen options.

The settings can be reverted to the state when the dialog was opened (Revert to initial settings) or completely to the factory settings (Reset to factory settings).
