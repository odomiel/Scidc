The Player List

The Player List shows all players occurring in the active database. For each player it shows, among other things, the frequency (number of games), federation, title, sex and the available ratings (Elo, DWZ and others).

The list can be sorted by any column. The search field filters by player name; the search is case-insensitive and matches substrings in any word order. Double-clicking opens the Player Card with statistics and biographical references.

The context menu offers actions such as filtering all games of a player and – if a link to the Player Dictionary exists – adopting verified master data (FIDE ID, title, federation).
