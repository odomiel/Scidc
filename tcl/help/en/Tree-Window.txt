INDEX Tree window
TITLE The Tree Window

<p>
The <b>Tree Window</b> shows, for the current board position, all continuation moves
occurring in the database together with their statistics. For each move it shows the
frequency, the score (success rate) and, where available, the average rating or year.
</p>

<p>
This makes it possible to see which moves are common in a position and how successful
they are &ndash; useful for opening preparation. Clicking a move plays it on the
board and updates the tree for the new position. The games belonging to a move can be
shown in the <a href="Tree-Games-Window.html">tree games list</a>.
</p>
