TITLE Welcome to Scidc!

<p>
Scidc is still in beta stage. Bugs and incomplete features are
possible &ndash; feedback is welcome.
</p>

<h2>Important Notes</h2>

<ul>
  <li class="space">
    The Scidb format (<abbr>.sci</abbr>/<abbr>.scv</abbr>) will no longer be developed
    further and is frozen at its current state.
    The new default format is the <abbr>.si5</abbr> format from Scid.
    However, support for it is still under development.
  </li>

  <li class="space">
    Support for the ChessBase format (<abbr>.cbh</abbr>) is also
    frozen at its current state and will not be extended further.
  </li>
</ul>
